# Rehab + Strength Master Plan (Buzz)

Version 2 · 25 Sep 2026 · Gym: Anytime Fitness · Machines and cables only (no free weights)

This file holds everything the app needs: injuries, rules, stages, sessions, exercise library, starting weights and progression logic. Not medical advice; check stage changes with your physio.

**Three priorities, in order**
1. Knee and wrist rehab in every session
2. Glute and hamstring strength (long-standing weak point)
3. Build everything else: chest, back, arms, abs

---

## 1. Injury profile

**Knee: patellar tendinopathy**
- Overloaded tendon below the kneecap. Treated with progressive loading, not rest.
- Status: isometric phase done, starting Stage 2 (heavy slow resistance).
- Related: left-side overpronation (orthotics), McConnell taping for patellar tracking.

**Right wrist: TFCC injury**
- TFCC (triangular fibrocartilage complex) = the cartilage and ligament cushion on the pinky side of the wrist. It stabilises the joint between the two forearm bones (the DRUJ, distal radioulnar joint).
- Recurring since teens, current episode about 6 months. Sharp pinky-side pain on twisting and gripping, aching and swelling after use. Six-week splint trial done.
- Rehab target: strengthen the two muscles that stabilise the DRUJ: pronator quadratus (PQ, deep forearm muscle that turns the palm down) and extensor carpi ulnaris (ECU, pinky-side wrist extensor).

**Left shoulder: subacromial bursitis + AC joint degeneration + posterior cuff weakness**
- Subacromial bursitis = irritated fluid sac under the top of the shoulder, pinched when the arm goes overhead or out to the side.
- AC joint = where collarbone meets shoulder blade. Flares with arms crossing the body and deep pressing.
- Rule: no shoulder training. Optional low-load cuff care only (Section 7), physio-approved.

**Glutes and hamstrings: long-standing weakness**
- Not an injury, a priority. Two glute or hamstring machines in every session, placed early while fresh.

**Lower back: previous history**
- No loaded spinal bending, no heavy weight pressing down through the spine.

## 2. Global rules

- **Machines and cables only.** No barbells, no dumbbells. Rehab bands and grip putty are the only extras.
- **Pain scale 0 to 10.** Knee and wrist pain during any set: 3/10 max. Shoulder: 2/10 max.
- **24-hour rule:** next-morning pain or swelling no worse than baseline.
- **RIR** (reps in reserve) = reps you could still do at the end of a set. Target 2 to 3. No sets to failure during rehab.
- **Tempo 3-0-3** = 3 sec lowering, no pause, 3 sec lifting. Knee strength exercises only.
- **Wrist:** neutral grip (palms facing each other) where possible. Lifting straps for heavy pulls. No weight on a bent-back wrist (no push-ups, no hand planks). No loaded twisting.
- **Shoulder banned:** overhead press, lateral raise, upright row, chest fly or pec deck, dips, behind-neck pulldown, push-ups.
- **Knee banned (Stage 2):** lunges, jumping, deep squats, hack squat, hills.
- **Back banned:** deadlifts, barbell squats, loaded sit-ups.
- **Gym days:** 3 per week, never back to back (tendons need 48 hours to adapt).
- **Home days:** 10 to 15 min rehab block (Section 8).

## 3. Rehab stages

### Knee

Based on the Kongsgaard heavy slow resistance protocol (3 sessions a week, reps falling as load rises over 12 weeks) and the Malliaras loading progression.

- **Stage 1: Isometric** (complete). Wall sit or Spanish squat 5 x 45 sec.
- **Stage 2: Heavy slow resistance, weeks 1 to 12**
    - Weeks 1 to 2: 3 x 15
    - Weeks 3 to 4: 3 x 12
    - Weeks 5 to 6: 4 x 10
    - Weeks 7 to 9: 4 x 8
    - Weeks 10 to 12: 4 x 6
    - Load = heaviest weight that hits target reps at 2 RIR with pain 3/10 or less.
- **Stage 3: Spring loading** (skipping, pogo hops, low box jumps). Unlocks when:
    - 8+ weeks of Stage 2 done
    - Single-leg decline squat pain 2/10 or less
    - Leg press 4 x 6 with no morning flare for 2 weeks
    - Hack squat returns here at 40 kg
- **Stage 4: Return to running.** Flat first, hills later, downhills last.
- **Weekly score:** VISA-P (standard 8-question knee tendon score out of 100; higher = better).

### Wrist

Advance only after **14 days in a row** of wrist pain 2/10 or less. Drop back one stage if pain goes above 3/10 or swelling appears next day.

- **W1: Isometric** (start here). Push against resistance without moving. Forearm thumb-up, 30% effort, 5 to 10 sec holds, 3 x 10.
- **W2: Band strength.** Light resistance band through pain-free range, 3 x 10 to 15.
- **W3: Load bearing.** Wall push-up holds, weight shifts, ball catches, twisting against resistance.
- **Weekly score:** PRWE (standard 15-question wrist score out of 100; lower = better).

### Glutes and hamstrings (strength milestones)

- **Milestone 1:** hip thrust back to pre-injury 70 kg x 10
- **Milestone 2:** lying hamstring curl back to pre-injury 47.5 kg x 12
- **Milestone 3:** hip thrust 100 kg x 10 (roughly bodyweight-plus, a solid glute strength marker)
- **Milestone 4:** hip abduction 70 kg x 12

## 8. Rehab blocks

### Wrist block (every gym session and home day, 8 to 10 min)

**W1**
- Palm-down isometric (PQ): forearm thumb-up, try to turn palm down against a door handle or your other hand, no movement, 30% effort, 3 x 10 x 5 to 10 sec
- Palm-up isometric: same setup, turn palm up, 3 x 10 x 5 to 10 sec
- Pinky-side isometric (ECU): forearm on table palm down, lift the pinky side of the hand up and out against your other hand, gentle, 3 x 10 x 5 sec
- Grip putty squeeze, softest putty, 3 x 10 x 5 sec
- Dart-throw motion (wrist moves from back-and-thumb-side to front-and-pinky-side, like throwing a dart), no load, 2 x 15

**W2**
- Band palm-down/palm-up turns, elbow at 90 degrees, light band, 3 x 10 to 15
- Band ECU lift: palm down, wrist slightly back, lift toward pinky side, 3 x 15
- Band wrist extension and flexion, forearm supported, 3 x 15
- Grip putty, next resistance, 3 x 10 x 5 sec
- Dart-throw motion with light band, 2 x 15

**W3**
- Wall push-up hold, 3 x 10 x 5 sec
- Hands-and-knees weight shifts, 2 x 10
- Ball throw and catch into right hand: palm up, then thumb up, then palm down, 3 x 30
- Twist against band resistance through full rotation, 3 x 10
- Gradually stop using straps on rows

### Home rehab day (10 to 15 min)

- Knee: Spanish squat or wall sit, 5 x 45 sec, 2 min rest
- Wrist block at current stage
- Floor glute bridge, bodyweight, 2 x 15 (glute activation, no equipment)
- Walk 20 to 30 min, flat

## 9. Progression logic (for the app)

**Knee lifts (leg extension, leg press)**
- Sets and reps from the current Stage 2 week.
- Increase next session if ALL true: all reps hit, last set RIR 2+, pain 3/10 or less, morning check same or better. Leg extension +2.5 kg, leg press +5 kg.
- When reps drop to the next block (e.g. 15 to 12), raise load about 8%, rounded to machine increment.
- Bad morning check: next session -20% load, 3 sets.
- Two bad mornings in a row: isometric-only for 3 to 4 days, then restart at reduced load (flare mode).

**Glute, hamstring and upper body lifts (double progression)**
- Double progression = add reps within the range first, then add weight.
- All sets at top of range with RIR 2+: add the exercise increment, drop back to bottom of range.
- Below bottom of range 2 sessions running: -10% load.
- Glute and hamstring lifts progress fastest; they carry no injury gate unless knee or back pain appears.

**Pain flags and swaps**
- Wrist pain above 3/10 or shoulder above 2/10 on any set: hold load, flag exercise, offer swap.
- Chest press flares shoulder: shorten range; still sore, drop for the week.
- Row or pulldown flares wrist: straps; still sore, single-arm with straps.
- Curl or pushdown flares wrist: -30% load; still sore, switch to isometric hold at 90 degrees elbow.

**Gates**
- Wrist: 14 days in a row at 2/10 or less unlocks next stage.
- Knee: Stage 3 criteria in Section 3.

**Morning knee check:** 5 slow single-leg squats to a chair (or one flight of stairs down), pain 0 to 10 per leg.

## 10. Recovery support

- Protein high while in a calorie deficit; tendons adapt more slowly when under-fuelled.
- Optional: 15 g gelatin or collagen with vitamin C, 30 to 60 min before gym. Modest evidence for tendon collagen building.
- Sleep 7+ hours; tendons remodel between sessions.
- Cadence 169 to 175 steps per minute when running returns (lowers knee tendon load).
